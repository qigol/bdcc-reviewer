## Conventions & ambiguities
- Currency: the lecture uses ₱; the case workbook prints $. The module shows ₱ throughout with the numbers unchanged.
- The deck rounds per-unit figures to the cent (18.88, 6.56, 20.73); the module computes unrounded and checks within ₱0.005.
- "Machine handling" on slide 27 is "Material handling" on slide 30 (its driver is weight of raw material); the module uses Material handling.
- GoldenCrisp's ABC overhead per Plain biscuit is 0.365 (slide: 0.37); the margins on the slide use the unrounded figure.

- **One signed convention (v1.1).** Every signed difference is traditional (plantwide) − ABC: + overcosted, − undercosted. Job 107's schedule row and the single-rate derivation therefore show 13,125 − 37,750 = −24,625. Where a question wants the size of the undercosting it says so and asks for a positive amount (`gap` = ABC − plantwide = 24,625, slide 32's figure, kept in examples; `diff` = −24,625).
- **plantQ (v1.1).** Pools 1–2 are drawn only from non-machine-hour drivers (audit C1: two contradictory machine-hour pools in 107 of 300 seeds), and draws where the single rate does not undercost the job are rejected, so plant-handcalc can ask for the undercosting as a positive amount.
- **Generator misconceptions (v1.1)** are keyed with `for:` to the answer var they produce (rate, abcTotal, plantOH, plantRate, abcLo, tradRate, tradLo, diffLo, diffHi, pctTrad, perVisit3, loserProfit, oRate, dRate, loserCost, and the new marginQ/bidQ vars).
- **Pool levels (v1.1).** The scene-4 sort classifies only pools whose level is clear: Global Metals' setups and inspection (batch), its other overhead per machine hour (unit), Laguna's machining (unit) and setups (batch). Material handling and waste control (per lb) are left out because their level depends on how the material moves. These level labels are this module's, not the slides'.
- **Scene 2 walk.** The Job 107 player stops after the job total (`stop: 6`) so the per-tool figure can be asked; the next beat hides the player and sets `sheetUpTo: 10` to show the plantwide comparison.
- **Services vs customers (v1.1).** Customer profitability has its own scene and skill (`customer-profit`), so the customers Math section is placed after the scene that defines gross margin, cost to serve and operating profit.
- **Scene 5 walk (v1.3).** The diagnostic center has no single-rate base, so `compareCore` ends with a "compared" step on the cost rows, which the scene does not show. The player passes `stop: 5` (three rates, the overhead each line uses, the per-visit row) and maps only the `rates` and `assign` roles; example `cavite-dx-walk` expects 5 steps.
- **Job 105 predict (v1.3).** The gated beat only sets up the comparison; the two-stage sentence, the departmental-rate sentence, both defines and the rates table open on the next beat (§7.1b).
- **Option feedback (v1.3).** Every lesson predict MCQ and Application MCQ decision has `optionFeedback` for each wrong option, keyed by authored index (the engine keeps the keys through shuffling).
- **Caselet templates (v1.3).** `caselet-simple-numeric` (CleanWave, Herbal ABC overhead per bar ₱4.80), `caselet-moderate-numeric` (GoldenCrisp, Assorted Gift Pack ABC full cost ₱12.35 against a ₱9.00 price) and `caselet-complex-numeric` (Radiance, Herbal Conditioner undercosted ₱3.86 a unit, the caselet's "flatter most" question) are static templates with literal answers recomputed with `caselet()`. They replace the generic compare templates as the caselets section's tryIt. The Radiance prompt asks for a positive undercosting; the negative of the answer is a keyed misconception.
- **Account titles (X11).** This session has no journal entries. The glossary's factory-overhead entry names the other modules' titles: Factory Overhead (job-order, process-costing) and Manufacturing Overhead (cost-flows).

## Errata / inconsistencies found
- Slide 39 is titled "Four Levels of Activity" but lists only unit, batch and product levels; facility-level appears on slides 41–42.
- Slide 44 says the chair is overcosted "‒42% relative to its true ABC cost" and the cabinet "+108% relative to its true ABC cost". Both percentages are relative to the **traditional** cost (25 ÷ 60 = 41.7%, 260 ÷ 240 = 108.3%); relative to ABC they are 71.4% and 52%. The signs are also reversed relative to the "T vs ABC" column.
- Slide 36's "Basic Bins subsidizes Custom Containers by ₱33/unit" mixes bases: Bins overpay ₱6.60 per bin (₱330,000 in total), which is ₱33.00 per container.
- Slide 99 (Apex) says Tier B needs "0.3" proposals per engagement won; the data table gives 2 per win (60 proposals for 30 engagements).
- Slide 12 says two rates changed Job 105's cost "by 15%": (289.00 − 245.80) ÷ 289.00 = 14.9%, or 17.6% of the plantwide figure.
