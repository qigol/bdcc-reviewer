## Worked examples (exact numbers)
- U.S. CSD cases sold: 10,377 million (2004) to 8,520 million (2018)
- U.S. CSD share 1940: 53 percent; 1960: 37 percent, Pepsi 18 percent
- Pepsi volume in Dallas grew 14 percent after the 1975 ads vs 6 percent the year before
- Diet Coke 1982 with a 100 million dollar advertising plan; third largest CSD by end of 1983
- Revenue 44.3 billion dollars (2015) to 34.3 billion (2018); headcount 123,200 to 62,600; operating margin 19.7 percent to 26.7 percent
- Quincey cut 1,200 of 5,500 Atlanta headquarters positions in 2017
- About 43 countries tax sugary drinks; Philadelphia taxed 1.5 cents per ounce from 1 January 2017; taxed beverage sales fell 51 percent in the first year, 38 percent adjusted for cross-border purchases
- New products rose from about 15 percent of revenue (2017) to nearly 25 percent (2019); 30 percent of products made 1 percent of volume; over 1,300 zombies removed in 2018 and 2019
- Costa: 5.1 billion dollars, 1.7 billion revenue, 3,800 cafes in 32 countries, 16.4 times 2018 EBITDA vs Starbucks at 13 times
- Water use fell from 2.7 liters to 1.85 liters per liter of beverage (2004 to 2019); replenished 81 billion liters (2012) to 273.7 billion liters (2019)
- Goals: 100 percent recyclable packaging by 2025; collect and recycle one bottle for every bottle sold by 2030
- 2019: revenue 37.3 billion dollars; dividend 1.60 dollars per share, 57th straight yearly increase; 80 percent of volume sold outside the U.S.
