## Worked examples (exact numbers)
- Over 202 million expatriates worldwide in 2024 — slide 3
- U.S. has 4 percent of the world's population but over 18 percent of its migrants; two-thirds of migrants live in developed countries — slide 11
- Typical assignment two to five years; one-quarter leave the company during the assignment — slides 29-30
- Up to 90 percent of expatriate failures are family related; over 75 percent who decline relocations cite family; only about one in five companies require cross-cultural training — slide 33
- About one-quarter of expats have dependent children — slide 35
- In practice 65 to 75 percent of base salary is paid in host-country currency — slide 45

## Conventions & ambiguities
- Learning objective 14-2 says "global" for the fourth approach while slide 18 says "geocentric". The module uses "geocentric" and notes the pairing with the transnational strategy.
- Slide 20 pairs ethnocentric with "primarily international strategic orientation" and the learning objective with "home replication"; the module treats them as the same idea.
- Figures 14.1 to 14.5 are images; only the text on the slides is used.
