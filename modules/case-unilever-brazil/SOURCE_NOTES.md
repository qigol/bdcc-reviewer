## Worked examples (exact numbers)
- Detergent powder in the Northeast: 106 million dollars, 42,000 tons, growing 17 percent a year; soap 102 million dollars, 81,250 tons, growing 6 percent
- Unilever Northeast powder share 75 percent (81 percent national); Omo 52 percent at 3 dollars per kg; Minerva 17 percent at 82 percent of Omo's price (2.40); Campeiro 6 percent at 57 percent of Omo's price (1.70); P&G Ace 11 percent (2.40)
- Laundry soap: Minerva 19.1 percent at 1.70 per kg (41 percent below Omo); Bem-te-vi 11.3 percent at 1.20; Flora 6.0 percent at 1.20; top four players hold 38 percent
- Exhibit 10 (S sales in million dollars, WP wholesale price, FC formulation, PKC packaging, PC promotion; all per kg except S): Omo S 55.12 WP 3.00 FC 1.65 PKC 0.35 PC 0.35; Minerva powder S 17.60 WP 2.40 FC 1.40 PKC 0.35 PC 0.30; Campeiro S 6.05 WP 1.70 FC 0.90 PKC 0.35 PC 0.20; Ace S 11.80 WP 2.35; Bold S 5.35 WP 2.50; Pop S 1.40 WP 1.70; Invicto S 5.20 WP 1.70; Minerva soap S 19.40 WP 1.70 FC 1.00 PKC 0.15 PC 0.25; Bem-te-vi S 11.45 WP 1.15
- Unit margins (author's arithmetic): Omo 0.65, Minerva powder 0.35, Campeiro 0.25, Minerva soap 0.30 dollars per kg
- Brand cost assumptions: new brand adds 0.10 dollars per kg of marketing cost, brand extension 0.05, repositioning 0
- Communication: regular plan 70 percent above the line and 30 percent below; a 70 percent below-the-line plan costs 0.05 dollars per kg, one third of a traditional plan
- Distribution: about 75,000 small outlets; generalist wholesaler variable cost 0.10 dollars per kg, specialized distributors 0.05
- Region facts: income per capita 2,250 dollars (Northeast) vs 6,600 (Southeast); illiteracy 40 vs 15 percent; 53 vs 21 percent live on under two minimum wages; Recife washing machine ownership 28 percent vs Sao Paulo 67 percent; bleach necessary for fat stains 73 vs 18 percent; washes per week 5 vs 3.9
- Attribute importance: cleanliness, whitening, productivity 24; smell and softness 20; stain removal 16; dissolving 16; packaging 13; harm to colors 11 (percent)
- Nirma in India: 48 percent share, Unilever 24 percent; detergent penetration 95 percent in Brazil vs 55 percent in India

## Conventions & ambiguities
- Exhibit text is partly scrambled in the extraction. Brand names for Exhibit 10 and the generalist vs specialized columns of Exhibit 13 were verified from page images.
- The case text says the specialized distributors give exclusive rights for all Unilever detergents in certain areas. The module treats the 0.05 per kg figure as the specialized distributors' variable cost to reach the smallest stores.
- The case ends before any decision; no outcome after 1996 is stated in this module.
