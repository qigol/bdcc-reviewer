## Formulas (verbatim, as TeX)
- None. Peso value of a dollar order = dollars x rate. Covered interest rate parity (words only in the notes): the percentage difference between the forward and spot rates of two currencies equals the difference in nominal interest rates.

## Worked examples (exact numbers)
- Cebu furniture exporter, order of USD 100,000, 90 days. Rate 58 per dollar, budget 5.8 million pesos. On settlement the peso is 55, so 5.5 million arrive: 300,000 less than budget (Supplemental slide 3).
- Hedge outcome table (rate on payday 55 / 58 / 61): Wait 5.5 / 5.8 / 6.1 million; Lock it in 5.8 / 5.8 / 5.8; Insurance (option, fee 100,000) 5.7 / 5.7 / 6.0. Need at least 5.6 million for payroll. Checked against the slide image (page 17 of the PDF): Option = max(5.8, market value) minus the 0.1 million fee.
- Multilateral netting (Figure 15.4 note): eight transactions and 1.2 million dollars in transit without netting; four transactions and 600,000 dollars with netting — slide 19 notes.
- Unilever 2025 (Supplemental): sells in 190 countries; emerging markets 59 percent of sales; underlying sales growth 3.5 percent (1.5 volume, 2.0 price); reported turnover down 3.8 percent to 50.5 billion euros; currency alone cut 5.9 points from turnover growth and 8.8 points from underlying EPS; prices rose 2.0 percent group-wide.
- Coca-Cola 2026 (Supplemental): sold in more than 200 countries; 84 percent of 2025 volume outside the U.S. (10-K 2025); about 61 percent of revenue outside the U.S. in the first nine months of 2026, 24.0 billion of 39.2 billion dollars; currency added about 2 points to Q3 EPS growth; about 3 percent boost expected for full-year comparable EPS, a slight drag in 2027.
