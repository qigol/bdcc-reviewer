## Conventions & ambiguities
- The slides use both "segment scanning" (definition) and "segment screening" (criteria). The module treats them as one idea and uses "segment scanning" as the glossary term.
- Order of the five screenings follows Figure 12.1: least to most difficult by data access and subjectivity (speaker note, slide 3).
- Plain-language gloss of the five segment criteria is the author's, not the slides' (marked beyond the slides where it appears).
