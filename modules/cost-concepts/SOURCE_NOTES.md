## Conventions & ambiguities
- **Classification of the Controller and Treasurer in Example 6.** The slide's solution lists six roles (four information, two money). The module also places the Controller itself on the information side and the Treasurer on the money side, as slide 26 defines them.
- **Direct against indirect for the second cost object.** The deck classifies the four items for the tables only, noting chair fabric is "direct to chairs". For the second column the module applies the same rule symmetrically: wood used in a table order is indirect to chairs, and building depreciation and the supervisor are indirect to both.
- **Account titles in the flow.** The deck names no ledger accounts for the product-cost flow (slide 60 uses words such as "Work-in-Process inventory"). The module's entries use the job-order module's titles: Raw Materials Inventory, Wages Payable, Factory Overhead, Work in Process, Finished Goods, Cost of Goods Sold.
- **Currency.** The peso (₱) throughout; the deck's "1:10:100" slide has ₱ signs that the PDF text layer drops.
