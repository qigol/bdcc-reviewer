## Formulas (verbatim, as TeX)
- POHR: `\text{POHR} = \frac{\text{Budgeted Annual Overhead}}{\text{Budgeted Annual Activity}}` — slide 29
- Applied overhead: `\text{Applied OH} = \text{POHR} \times \text{Actual activity incurred on the job}` — slide 29
- Underapplied: `\text{Applied OH} < \text{Actual OH}`; overapplied: `\text{Applied OH} > \text{Actual OH}` — slide 40
- Gap: `\text{Actual overhead} - \text{Applied overhead}` — slide 41
- Sales price at markup: `\text{Cost} + m \times \text{Cost}` — slide 22
- COGS/FG identity used on slide 62: `\text{End. FG} = \text{Beg. FG} + \text{COGM} - \text{COGS}`
- EVPI (aside): `\text{EVPI} = \text{EPPI} - \text{EMV}` — slide 38

## Conventions & ambiguities
- One overhead control account. Slide 61 names the apply-entry credit "Factory Overhead Applied", but slide 46 closes the difference against "Factory Overhead" and calls it the control account that the closing entry zeroes. A separate Applied account would need a different closing entry from the one the slides show, so the module credits **Factory Overhead** for applied overhead and closes the same account.
- Plantwide rate is rounded. (72,000 + 75,000) ÷ (64,000 + 17,500) = 180.37%; slide 53 uses 180% and gets 109.80 and 245.80. The module uses the slide's 180% (dataset `plantRate: 1.8`) and shows the exact figure in a note. The ₱17,500 of Department B budgeted labor cost appears only in Example 8.
- Overhead rates are expressed per the base: per DLH, per MH, or a percentage of DL cost; applied OH = rate × the job's actual base.
- Proration weights are the applied overhead remaining in each account at year end (slide 43), not total account balances.
- Marikina's estimated overhead (₱30,000) is implied: ₱120,000 estimated cost − ₱50,000 DM − ₱40,000 DL.
- Each time ticket keeps its own wage rate (Chiphard: 6.50 then 7.25).
- Gross margin percent on slide 59 is GM ÷ price.

## Errata
- slide 34: Company Y's budget is printed as ₱160,000 of overhead on ₱160,000 of direct labor cost, which is 100%, yet the rate is stated and used as 150%. The module follows the solution's 150% (applied 264,000; overapplied 8,000) and shows an errata callout.
- slide 73: the "25% margin" on the quote is a 25% markup on the ₱120,000 estimated cost; on price the planned margin was ₱30,000 ÷ ₱150,000 = 20%. The slide then compares the actual 18.7% (on price) with 25% (on cost). The correct comparison is 20.0% → 18.7%.
- slide 73: "The shortfall traces almost entirely to direct materials" — with the implied ₱30,000 overhead estimate, DM +8,000, DL +2,000 and OH −8,000 net to +2,000; the conclusion that the materials estimate needs review still holds.
